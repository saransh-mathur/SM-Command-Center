1. **Physiological Sigh & Vagus Nerve Regulation:**

2. **Visual & Attentional Framing:**
   - Use narrow visual gaze (focal screen/terminal framing) to trigger alertness via acetylcholine release.
3. **Dopamine Baseline & Friction Management:**
   - Shift from outcome-based reward to effort-based reward.

4. **Ultradian Cycles & NSDR:**
   - Structure work into 90-minute hyper-focus blocks followed by 10–20 minute Non-Sleep Deep Rest (NSDR) or low-stimulation breaks.
